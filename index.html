Japan

Japan is an East Asian island nation in the Pacific Ocean, primarily comprising four main islands—Honshu, Hokkaido, Kyushu, and Shikoku—with a mountainous landscape dominated by iconic landmarks like Mount Fuji. Its capital, Tokyo, sits at the center of one of the world's most populous and technologically advanced metropolitan areas. Renowned for seamlessly blending ancient traditions with modern innovation, Japan pairs centuries-old temples, tea ceremonies, and rich culinary arts with cutting-edge infrastructure, global leadership in technology, and influential pop culture like anime and gaming.
